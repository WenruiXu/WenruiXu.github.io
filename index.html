Hello :)

My name is Wenrui Xu, and I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the College of Science and Engineering, University of Minnesota Twin Cities, where I am advised by Prof. Keshab K. Parhi.

I received my Bachelor of Computer Engineering from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities in 2023. My research interests span machine learning, large language models, representation learning, and hyperdimensional computing, with a focus on time-series data analysis and AI security.

Research Interest

  • Large Language Models: studying attacks, risks, and practical safeguards for modern language models.
  • Machine Learning in Biomedical Field: developing time-series data analysis models for ECG/EEG signals, arrhythmia/seizure classification, and clinical outcome prediction.
  • Representation Learning: learning compact and useful data representations for downstream prediction and analysis tasks.
  • Hyperdimensional Computing: developing efficient feature selection, outlier detection, and classification methods.
  • Knowledge Graphs: exploring structured representations for domain-aware reasoning and retrieval.
